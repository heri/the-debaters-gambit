# The Debater’s Gambit — Product Strategy

## From Card Game to Daily Habit: A Duolingo-Informed Roadmap

-----

## The Core Problem We’re Solving

Kids default to three arguing strategies: repeating themselves louder, crying, or “that’s not fair.” This isn’t stubbornness — they simply don’t have the **vocabulary of persuasion** yet. The Debater’s Gambit gives them that vocabulary, disguised as a game. The goal of the app isn’t just engagement. It’s transferable skill. The day a child uses “The Flattery Trap” on their parent without even realising it — that’s the win.

-----

## The Duolingo Lessons (What We Steal, What We Skip)

### ✅ What works — and why it maps to us

|Duolingo Mechanic          |Why It Works                                                               |Our Version                                                     |
|---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
|**Streaks**                |Loss aversion. Kids hate breaking a streak more than they love starting one|“Debate Streak” — daily argument = streak maintained            |
|**XP & Levelling**         |Quick visible progress. Small wins release dopamine                        |“Wit Points” — earned per session, debate, and solo drill       |
|**Bite-sized sessions**    |5 min feels doable. Creates habit before resistance forms                  |Daily “Quick Draw” = 1 case + 1 move card, under 5 min          |
|**Mascot with personality**|Duo the owl guilt-tripping you is a meme for a reason                      |Our mascot: a sharp-dressed fox called **Argus**                |
|**Tiered progression**     |Visible path forward. Always know what’s next                              |Bronze → Silver → Gold → Grandmaster tiers                      |
|**Failure feels safe**     |Wrong answers = gentle animation, not red screen                           |“Good try! Here’s what a stronger argument sounds like…”        |
|**Sound + animation**      |Multi-sensory = memorable. Kids associate sound with reward                |Signature sounds per move card. Card-flip animation. Crowd cheer|

### ❌ What we skip

|Duolingo Mechanic                 |Why We Skip It                                                                     |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|**Aggressive daily notifications**|Great for retention, bad for parents. We do 1 gentle nudge max, parent-controlled  |
|**Leaderboards (global)**         |Wrong incentive for this age — can cause shame. Replace with **Family League** only|
|**Ads**                           |Kills trust with parents. Freemium only                                            |

## The Four Pillars of the App

### 🎮 Pillar 1 — SOLO DOJO (Train Alone)

*The single-player learning mode. This is your Duolingo lesson.*

A move card is presented. Instead of just reading the description, the kid is **walked through it in steps**, like a mini coaching session.

**Example — “The Magnifying Glass” Drill:**

> **Step 1 — LISTEN** 🎧
> *“Argus says: ‘Here’s what your opponent just argued. Listen carefully…’”*
> [Audio plays: “We should have longer recess because kids are happier outside!”]
> 
> **Step 2 — SPOT THE HOLE** 🔍
> *“What did they NOT prove? Tap the weak spot.”*
> [Multiple choice: A) They didn’t say WHY happiness matters / B) They’re wrong about outside / C) Recess is already long enough]
> 
> **Step 3 — BUILD THE COUNTER** 🛠️
> *“Now say it back! Fill in the blank…”*
> “You said kids are happier outside — but being happy doesn’t automatically mean ___”
> [Kid types or picks: “we learn more / it helps school / recess should be longer”]
> 
> **Step 4 — DELIVER IT** 🎤
> *“Now say it OUT LOUD like you mean it. You have 10 seconds.”*
> [Timer + recording option OR just a “I did it!” tap]
> 
> ⭐ **+15 Wit Points. Combo unlocked: Magnifying Glass x2!**

Each move card has its own 4-step drill. Kids can replay them. Each replay gets slightly harder (more complex scenarios, fewer multiple choice hints).

-----

### ⚔️ Pillar 2 — THE ARENA (Play with Someone)

*The two-player live game — the original Debater’s Gambit, digitised.*

This is the **heart** of parent-child play.

**How it flows in the app:**

1. One device, passed back and forth (or two devices on same session)
1. App randomly draws a Case Card + assigns Pro/Con
1. Each player gets 2 Move Cards (shown only to them, face-down until played)
1. Built-in **timer** with audio cues (30s opening, 15s closing)
1. Judge mode: big clear YES/NO/DRAW buttons + optional “Why did you pick this?” journal prompt
1. After judgment: app shows **what move cards each player had** — great conversation starter (“Oh! You had The Feint and you didn’t use it!”)

**Parent Mode unlocks:** After the game, parent gets a prompt — “Want to show them how you’d use The Dreamer? Go!” Reverse the roles. Model the skill.

-----

### 📚 Pillar 3 — THE VAULT (Collection & Progress)

*The collector’s dopamine hit.*

Kids collect things. This is non-negotiable.

- **Move Card Collection**: Each card is a collectible. Locked cards shown as silhouettes. Unlock by completing Solo Dojo for that card.
- **Case Card Collection**: Unlock new cases by playing Arena games. Cases have rarity (Common, Rare, Legendary 🌟).
- **Combo Book**: When a kid uses a specific Case + Move Card combo, it gets “stamped” in their Combo Book. 150 combos total to discover. This is the Pokédex mechanic.
- **Argus Reactions**: The fox mascot reacts differently based on what move you used. Kids will replay just to see all his reactions.
- **Trophy Shelf**: Visible badges — “First Win vs. a Parent 🏆”, “Used The Dreamer 3 times in a row 🌟”, “Completed the Gold Dojo 🥇”

-----

### 🌅 Pillar 4 — DAILY QUICK DRAW (The Habit Loop)

*5 minutes. Every day. This is the streak machine.*

Every morning, the app generates a **Daily Quick Draw** — a combination of:

- 1 randomly drawn Case Card (age-appropriate, rotating daily)
- 1 Move Card to practise
- A Solo Dojo micro-drill for that Move Card (2 min max)

Completing the Daily Quick Draw = streak maintained + bonus Wit Points.

**Streak Rewards at milestones:**

- 3 days: Unlock “Argus Coach” avatar costume
- 7 days: New Legendary Case Card revealed
- 14 days: “Silver Tongue” badge
- 30 days: Unlock Silver tier Move Cards
- 60 days: Gold tier unlocks begin

The streak is **family-shared** — parents can see it on their parent dashboard. This creates a natural “did you do your Quick Draw today?” conversation that feels less like nagging homework and more like checking a game score.

## The Progression Path (Full Arc)

```
🟢 EASY TIER (Ages 6-8)
   Easy Move Cards only (Fair Play, Kind Heart, etc.)
   Kid Cases K01-K10 only
   Solo Dojo: fully guided, audio-heavy, big buttons
   Arena: parent always judges
   ↓
🥉 BRONZE TIER (Ages 8-10)
   Unlocks Bronze Move Cards
   Unlocks full Case Card library
   Solo Dojo: fewer hints, fill-in-the-blank style
   Arena: can play against older siblings
   ↓
🥈 SILVER TIER (Ages 10-12)
   Unlocks Silver Move Cards
   Solo Dojo: open-ended response mode ("say it your way")
   Arena: multi-round format, best of 3
   New mechanic: REBUTTAL ROUND added
   ↓
🥇 GOLD TIER (Ages 12+)
   Unlocks Gold Move Cards
   Solo Dojo: "spot the flaw in THIS argument" — AI-generated arguments to dismantle
   Arena: timed speed rounds
   New mechanic: DOUBLE MOVE — play 2 cards in one turn
   ↓
🏆 GRANDMASTER
   All cases, all cards
   "Challenge a Parent" mode
   Custom Case Card creator
   Invite friends outside family
```

## The Parent Layer (Critical for Adoption)

Parents are the **distribution channel**. If a parent feels this is genuinely developing their child, they download it, they keep it installed, and they tell other parents. Design for them explicitly.

**Parent Dashboard (separate view):**

- Child’s streak + last session summary
- Which Move Cards they’ve mastered vs. struggling with
- “Play Together” shortcut to launch Arena
- Notification settings (1 gentle nudge max, time-of-day controlled by parent)
- Weekly “Debate Digest” — a 3-sentence summary: “This week Maya mastered The Magnifying Glass and used it twice in Arena. She’s ready to try Silver tier. Suggested this week: play Case 07 together (Pitch an Invention).”

**Parent Journal Prompts** (after each Arena game):
These are light-touch, optional. After a game, one question appears for the parent:

> *“What was the most surprising thing your child argued today?”*
> Parents who answer unlock bonus Wit Points for their child. Builds the habit of reflection AND gives you UGC/testimonials.

## Notification Philosophy (Anti-Duolingo)

Duolingo’s aggressive notifications work for adults who opted in consciously. For kids aged 7-10, **parents control the device**. Aggressive notifications = parent deletes the app.

**Our approach:**

- **1 notification per day**, maximum, at a time the parent sets
- Notification comes from **Argus the fox**, not a system alert — personality matters
- Examples:
  - *“🦊 Argus here. Your Quick Draw is waiting. Today’s case: Should pizza be breakfast? I say yes.”*
  - *“🦊 5-day streak on the line! Argus believes in you. 3 minutes is all it takes.”*
- **Streak Freeze** mechanic (like Duolingo): kids earn 1 freeze per week. Teaches resource management AND reduces frustration on busy days.
- Parent can switch to **weekly digest only** mode — zero daily notifications, just a Sunday summary.

## MVP Scope (Version 1.0)

Build the habit loop first. Everything else is polish.

**Must have:**

- [ ] Solo Dojo for all Easy + Bronze Move Cards (12 cards × 4 steps = 48 micro-lessons)
- [ ] Arena mode (single device, pass-and-play)
- [ ] Daily Quick Draw with streak counter
- [ ] Kid Case Cards K01-K10 + Original Cases 01-20
- [ ] Basic card collection (unlock on completion)
- [ ] Argus mascot with 6-8 key reactions
- [ ] Parent dashboard (streak view + notification settings)
- [ ] 3 sound effects (card flip, win, streak maintained)

**Defer to V2:**

- Combo Book (150 combos)
- Silver + Gold Solo Dojo drills
- Multi-device Arena
- AI-powered argument generation for Gold Dojo
- Custom Case Card creator
- Parent Journal Prompts
- Sibling / friend invite

-----

## The One Metric That Matters

**Weekly Family Sessions** — how many times per week does at least one parent + one child play an Arena game together.

Everything else (streaks, Wit Points, collection) is in service of this number. A child who does Solo Dojo every day but never plays with a parent will gain skill but miss the point. A family that plays once a week, even without streaks, is experiencing the real product.

Target for V1: **2 Arena sessions per week per active family.**

The day a child, mid-argument with a parent about screen time or bedtime or whether they can have another biscuit, pauses — tilts their head — and says *“Actually, let me ask you something…”* and launches into a Socratic Endless Question…

That’s not the app working.
That’s the skill transferring.
That’s the win.
